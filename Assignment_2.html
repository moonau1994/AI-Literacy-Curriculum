Description

Assignment 1 – AI Use

For this assignment, you will interact with an AI chatbot about a topic that you do not know well. The goal is to see how well the AI can explain a topic to you, and to reflect on your experience. This topic should be sufficienty complex that you do not already know it well, but not so complex that it is impossible for the AI to explain. You will then answer a few reflection questions about your experience and upload screenshots of your interaction.

Requirements

There is no "right" or "wrong" way of completing this assignment; the goal is to interact with an AI chatbot about a topic that you understand.

  • At a minimum, ask the AI chatbot 7 prompts.
  • Once you have completed your conversation, take screenshots of the interaction.
  • Answer the survey questions, and upload the screenshots in the last question of the survey.
